How to read the numbers

  • Weights, prices and PEW Science ratings are published figures gathered October 7 and 8, 2026, rechecked against maker pages and major retailers. A tilde marks the few I could not verify.
  • Criteria tagged my rating are my 1 to 10 judgment where no published number exists (glass at dusk, toughness, stability). Set that slider to 0 to remove it from the score.
  • Scores are relative within each list: 100 means best in the list on every criterion you weighted. They change when you move a slider, not when you pick.
  • PEW Science tested every suppressor on a 20 in .308 bolt gun with M80 ball. Your 16.25 in barrel is louder with all of them, and the order should hold.
  • Ammunition reach is computed, not quoted: box velocity minus the short-barrel loss, through a G1 drag model at the altitude you set. No maker publishes a minimum expansion velocity for these loads except Hornady for CX.
