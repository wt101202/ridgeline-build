Christensen's lightest production action in a short, suppressor-ready barrel, in the black carbon stock with tan and green accents. The carbon barrel and stock are why the rest of this build stays light: every ounce added here is felt on the climb.

Where you hunt

Thin air carries a bullet farther, so sea level is the conservative case. Alaska coastal hunts are near sea level; a Colorado elk unit is 8,000 to 11,000 ft.

How you shoot

Position sets how much you wobble. Set how good your wind call is below; the range is assumed good to 5 yd.

Hit probability model

A 1 MOA rifle (0.3 MOA per-axis scatter), your position wobble, an aiming error that shrinks with magnification, your wind-call error in a 10 mph wind, and a 5 yd range error, all as normal distributions, integrated over the vital zone: 8 in on a coyote, 10 in on a deer, 14 in on an elk. Coyote reach is where the chance passes 50% and the bullet is still above 1,400 fps. Elk and deer reach also require the energy and expansion floors below.

Step 11

How to read the numbers

  • Weights, prices and ratings are published figures gathered October 7 to 12, 2026, rechecked against maker pages and major retailers. A tilde marks the few I could not verify.
  • Suppressor sound comes from two independent programs. PEW Science tests one can at a time on a 20 in .308 Savage with M80 ball and publishes a Suppression Rating that blends muzzle and ear measurements with impulse. The Thunder Beast Silencer Sound Summit tests dozens of brands in one session on a 20 in .308 bolt gun with M118LR, five shots each, on B&K microphones, and publishes A-weighted peak dB at the milspec positions and at a fixed shooter's-ear point. Lower dB is quieter; higher PEW rating is quieter. Neither has tested the SilencerCo Scythe-Ti, so it carries only SilencerCo's own figure.
  • Your 16.25 in barrel is louder with every can than the 20 in test hosts; the order should hold.
  • Criteria tagged my rating are my 1 to 10 judgment where no published number exists (glass at dusk, toughness, stability, solver). Set that slider to 0 to remove it from the score.
  • Scores are relative within each list: 100 means best in the list on every criterion you weighted. They change when you move a slider, not when you pick.
  • Renderings are drawn to each product's published length and diameter and the rifle's dimensions; they are not photographs, and finishes are approximations. Every card links to the maker's or dealer's listing for photos.
